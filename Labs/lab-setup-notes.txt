LLVM IR
1)Normal commands: clang -S -g -emit-llvm temp.c -o temp.ll 
clang -S -emit-llvm -Xclang -disable-O0-optnone temp.c -o temp.ll
2)Commands to view CFG:
clang -x c -S -emit-llvm -O0 -Xclang -disable-O0-optnone -fno-discard-value-names temp.c -o temp.ll
opt -passes=dot-cfg temp.ll -disable-output
